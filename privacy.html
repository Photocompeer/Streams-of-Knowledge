Privacy Policy

Protecting your data stream

What We Collect

  • Profile Data: Your Alias and School name to identify you in the lobby.
  • Performance Data: Your quiz scores, subject choices, and timestamps.
  • Authentication: We use Firebase to securely handle your login via email/password.

How Data is Used

Your data is strictly used to display your "Recent History" and manage the competitive rankings. We do not sell your academic performance data to third parties.

Data Security

All knowledge streams are encrypted via Google Firebase protocols. You can request to have your account and history deleted at any time by contacting our support team.
